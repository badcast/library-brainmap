Подробное описание

Hierarchical path finding (HPA*).

The map is cut into square clusters. Every free cell that has a legal step into another cluster is an "entrance" node of the abstract graph; the nodes of one cluster are linked by their exact in-cluster costs, the nodes of neighbour clusters by the border step. A query inserts the start and the goal into the graph, searches the small abstract graph and refines every abstract edge into real cells.

Because every border step is represented, the abstract graph is exact: the returned cost equals the cost of a Dijkstra search (the path itself may differ between equal-cost alternatives). The price is the memory and the build time of the graph, so the class pays off on big maps with many queries.

The graph stores its own data. It is built for the walls, the identity, the step costs and the corner cutting rule of the map at the moment of build(); call build() again after the map changes.

build the abstract graph

Аргументы
mapthe map to read, it is not modified
cluster_sizethe side of a square cluster (at least 2), bigger clusters mean a smaller abstract graph but slower queries
sparsefalse: every border step is represented, the graph is exact but big. true: only the first, the middle and the last step of every free stretch of a border are kept. The graph is several times smaller and much faster to build, the paths may be slightly longer than optimal, and when the sparse graph finds no route find() falls back to a plain A* search of the map, so reachability stays exact.
