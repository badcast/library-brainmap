Подробное описание

Flow Field (a.k.a.

Dijkstra map) toward one goal.

One Dijkstra run from the goal fills the whole map: every reachable cell gets the cost to the goal and the next cell on a cheapest path. After build() any number of agents can read their way in O(1) per step or extract a whole path, no search is needed. Use it when many units move to the same target.

The field uses the identity (neighbour matrix), the step costs, the locks and the corner cutting rule of the map at the moment of build(). Call build() again after the map changes. The field stores its own data, so the map can be searched while the field is alive.
